How to Play

1

The board starts with 14 pegs and one empty hole.

2

Drag a peg to jump over an adjacent peg into an empty hole. The jumped peg is removed.

3

Jumps can go in any of 6 directions — along the rows or diagonals of the triangle.

4

The goal is to leave as few pegs as possible. Leave just one and you're a damn genius!
